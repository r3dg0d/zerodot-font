FONTLOG for Zerodot
================
Zerodot: an original 5×7 dot-matrix display family. Every glyph is made of separate round dots, in 9 weights plus slanted italics.

Basic Font Information
----------------------
Generated procedurally by tools/engine.py from sources/dotmatrix.txt.
Engine: 5×7 matrix → one closed 4-curve dot contour per lit cell (never merged).
